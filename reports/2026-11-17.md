## Update (2026-11-18, on Jamel's request)

- New From My Shows only showed episodes from about a week ago. Cause: the Trakt provider's calendar slices the raw, oldest first response to pmax * 20 before filtering and reversing, and TMDb Helper caps pmax at pagemulti_trakt (1) for non-cacheonly widget paths, so length=8 was ignored and only 20 raw items (today-8 and part of today-7) survived. Long-standing since the 6.20 provider split, not a 6.22 regression. Reproduced with the released 6.22.1 and sync.trakt 1.3.0 classes.
- Draft PR #4, https://github.com/AANOTHERWORLD/KODI_PERSONAL/pull/4 (branch monitor/fix-2026-11-18, service 0.9.4): patch_trakt_calendar_limit adds limit = min(length or pmax, 8) * 20 to ListCalendarProperties on start and hourly; widgets without length are unchanged.
- PR #4 fast-forwarded to main at 26bb007 on Jamel's request. The repo index on main now serves service.kodipersonal.setup 0.9.4.
